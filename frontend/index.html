Help Center & FAQs
Frequently asked questions and user guides for customers & owners
How do I rent a vehicle as a customer?
Sign in with your customer account, click on Browse Vehicles, select your preferred dates, choose your payment method (UPI / Card / NetBanking), and confirm your booking. You will instantly get a transaction receipt!
How do vehicle owners manage their fleet and maintenance?
Vehicle owners can sign in using their Owner credentials. Once signed in, exclusive tabs will appear: Register Vehicle, Fleet Management, and the Maintenance Hub where you can record insurance policy dates, PUC expiry, service due dates, and vehicle health condition.
Are insurance and roadside support included?
Yes! All vehicles registered on Velocity Rentals undergo mandatory document inspection (Insurance & PUC). 24/7 Roadside Assistance is included for all customer rentals.
What payment methods are supported?
We accept UPI (GooglePay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa/Mastercard credit and debit cards, and NetBanking.
